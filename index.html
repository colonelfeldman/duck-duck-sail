How to Play

Welcome aboard! You start with so you can power up and promote your first crew straight away.

Catch

  • When a duck is nearby, tap the big button on the Deck.
  • Flick the life ring up at the duck. The direction you flick is where it goes; how fast you flick is how far. Too soft falls short, too hard flies over.
  • Ducks bob and dodge — aim where the duck will be.
  • Spin: circle the ring with your finger before you flick. Spin curves the throw. Enough spin is a Curveball: bonus XP and an easier reel.
  • The ring has to land on the duck: Excellent, Great or Nice. A miss splashes in the water.
  • Premium and Golden rings are easier to land and reel, and are used up on every throw. You get a Premium Ring each Voyager level.
  • Ducks can paddle away after too many misses.

Rescue reel

  • Once the ring is round its neck, hold to reel the duck in.
  • When it shows Struggling!, ease off. If the tension stays maxed out, the ring slips off.

Grow your crew

  • Each catch: and XP.
  • Power Up with Shells + Treats to raise a duck's level and Sail Power.
  • Promote with Duck Treats: Cruise Duck → Sailor Duck (25) → Captain Duck (100).
  • About 1 in 128 ducks is Gold.
  • Release a duck to the Harbormaster for +1 Duck Treat.

Signs

  • Scan a game sign to start an encounter there. The spot stays active for 30 minutes and changes which ducks appear.
  • Port signs add a stamp to your Passport.

Play safe

Stop walking before you catch. Play in public lounges and on open decks only — not on stairs, by the pool edge, or in crew areas.
